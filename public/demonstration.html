Interactive Optical Tracking Demonstration

Optical tracking uses ground-based telescopes to observe reflected sunlight from satellites and orbital debris, recording precise angular measurements as objects pass overhead. Combined with orbit determination techniques, these observations help build and refine a catalog of tracked objects — supporting collision avoidance, re-entry prediction, and broader space situational awareness efforts. The simulation below illustrates how a telescope mount rotates and tilts to follow a simulated target, alongside reference training material on the underlying data acquisition and analysis methods.
