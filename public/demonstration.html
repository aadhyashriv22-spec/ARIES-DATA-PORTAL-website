Interactive Demonstration

Interactive Optical Tracking Demonstration

This educational WebGL simulation illustrates how a ground-based optical telescope may follow a resident space object across the night sky.

ⓘ Simulation only — not connected to live telescope, satellite, or backend data.

How the Demonstration Works

◎ Step 1

Predicted Path

A simulated object moves along an illustrative orbital trajectory.

↻ Step 2

Telescope Alignment

The telescope mount rotates and tilts toward the simulated target.

↝ Step 3

Optical Line of Sight

A visual beam represents the telescope's observational direction.

☐ Step 4

Observation Capture

The demonstration illustrates how images and tracking measurements may be collected.

This visualization is an educational representation of optical tracking concepts. It does not display live observatory operations, real satellite positions, or current ARIES tracking data.
