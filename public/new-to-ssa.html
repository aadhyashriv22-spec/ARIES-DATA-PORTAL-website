Start Here

Space Situational Awareness (SSA) is the ability to detect, track, and understand objects in Earth orbit. As space gets more crowded, SSA is what keeps active satellites safe, predicts re-entries, and keeps orbit sustainable for everyone using it.

Observation

Telescopes and sensors detect objects in orbit.

→
Tracking

Repeated observations follow an object across its orbit.

→
Analysis

Observations combine into a precise orbit estimate.

→
Prediction

Future positions and risks are forecast ahead of time.
