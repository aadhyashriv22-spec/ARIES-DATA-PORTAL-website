About ARIES SSA

Observatories

Ground-based optical observation facilities supporting Space Situational Awareness tracking activities.

Satellite Tracking

Monitoring, characterization, and trajectory prediction of resident space objects orbiting Earth.

Data Repository

Calibrated scientific imagery, precision telemetry logs, observation sessions, and derived space products.

Research Collaboration

Secure observation sharing mechanisms for authorized research projects and partner academic institutions.

Latest Updates

2026-07-15

Latest Observation Campaign

Devasthal Optical Telescope successfully completed a coordinate tracking run of low-earth orbit communication satellites.

The tracking run spanned six low-Earth orbit communication satellites over three consecutive nights, validating pointing accuracy and data pipeline handoff ahead of the upcoming SSA observing season.

2026-07-02

Recently Added Dataset

Calibrated FITS observation imagery for orbital sector Leo-N2 has been indexed and is available for research download.

The Leo-N2 sector dataset includes calibrated FITS frames, astrometric solutions, and accompanying observation logs from the latest tracking pass.

Open in Data Portal →
2026-06-28

New SSA Collaboration

ARIES continues to expand its Space Situational Awareness partnership network with national space and defence research organisations.

This builds on ARIES's existing collaborations with ISRO, DRDO's Instruments Research & Development Establishment (IRDE), and Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL) — covering joint development of ground-based optical tracking systems, electro-optics research, and AI/ML-based image processing for space object monitoring.

Institutional Access

Access to the ARIES SSA Data Portal is restricted to authorized researchers and approved institutional collaborators.

Portal credentials are issued by the ARIES SSA Cell following approval of official research collaboration requests.
